Blackout Comms for Heltec v4 Expansion Kit

This is the firmware download page for installing Blackout Comms secure off-grid messaging and tracking to your Heltec v4 Expansion Kit. Other devices (not shown here) are also supported. For T-Deck and other devices, go to the main Blackout Comms Firmware Download page.
  1. Connect the Heltec to your PC using a high quality data cable.
  2. Press and hold the User button, tap the heltec's Reset button, then release the Reset button. It's now ready for a firmware flash.
  3. Select Communicator or Node below.
  4. Click the install button below and click through the steps.
