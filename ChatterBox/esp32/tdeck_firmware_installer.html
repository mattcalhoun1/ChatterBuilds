  1. Recommended - SD cards are highly recommended but not required. Only compatible SD cards can be used.
  2. Connect the T-Deck to your PC using a high quality data cable.
  3. Switch the T-Deck power on.
  4. On the T-Deck, press and hold the trackball button (left/first button), tap the reset button (on the side), then release the trackball button.
  5. Click the install button below and click throug the steps.
  6. In some cases, you may need to continue holding the trackball button while the install happens.
