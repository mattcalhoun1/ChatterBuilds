Your Private Off-Grid Mesh

Blackout Comms is a private off-grid mesh-based texting & tracking system that runs on LoRa hardware from Lilygo, Heltec, and Altware.

Versions, SD cards, and device notes

Current communicator firmware 3.7.8. Link firmware 3.7.6. SD cards are recommended. These boards are picky about cards — a known-good card avoids most failures.

  • T-Deck and T-Deck Plus use the communicator build.
  • Heltec v4 touchscreen uses the expansion-kit build.
  • Nodes and links use the link build.
